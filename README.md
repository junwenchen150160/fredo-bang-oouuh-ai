# Fredo Bang Oouuh AI Trend

The **Fredo Bang Oouuh AI** trend turns one or two photos into a short AI performance video based on the Oouuh-style scene.

Instead of filming and editing the performance yourself, you can upload photos and use AI to place yourself, a friend, or two people into the video while keeping the original movement, camera framing, and audio.

## What is the Fredo Bang Oouuh AI trend?

The trend is based on the Fredo Bang Oouuh performance format that has been circulating across short-form video platforms.

AI versions let people recreate the format with their own photos. A single photo can replace one person in the scene, while two photos can be used for a two-person version.

## How to make a Fredo Bang Oouuh AI video

1. Choose one or two clear portrait photos.
2. Upload the photos to an AI video generator that supports the Oouuh format.
3. Match each photo to the person you want to replace.
4. Generate the video.
5. Preview and download the finished MP4.

For two-person videos, use clear photos with visible faces so the AI can keep the two identities separate.

## One Photo vs Two Photos

**One photo:** replace one person in the performance.

**Two photos:** place two different people into the video while keeping their identities separate.

For better results, use front-facing photos with good lighting and avoid heavily covered or blurry faces.
